Kindness deserves a system.

People care deeply about the communities they serve. But care alone doesn’t get someone enrolled, or get a benefit to their door. Help only counts if it arrives, and good systems are how it arrives.

A note from our founder

Brendan KittFounder and Managing Director

I started my first community nonprofit in 2012. We ran after-school programs for kids at four low-income housing sites in Aurora, Illinois.

That’s where I fell in love with organizing, and where I started thinking about how to make support for families work as a system.

My career took me through corporate operations, Six Sigma and supply chain, then into food logistics and leading food procurement at Chicago’s food bank. I started Compago to bring all of that together and help organizations serve people better.

We are community people. We run our operations with the rigor of a highly competitive company. Good systems shouldn’t be limited to those with resources. Everyone deserves a strong system of support, and we help build it.

Brendan Kitt, Founder

What we value.

  • Care

    For the families we serve. It’s the reason the company exists, and it sets the standard for every call.

  • Stewardship

    Every dollar lost to broken execution is help that never reached someone. We treat efficiency as part of the mission.

  • Dignity

    A system isn’t good if it’s confusing or humiliating to use. We design every step around the person going through it.

Rigor runs through all three: the complete record, the accurate report, the follow-up call that actually happens.

The name.

Compago

Latin · a joining together

Compago comes from the Latin for joining together: a framework that binds parts into a whole.

That’s the work. Enrollment, fulfillment, and reporting, joined into one system, so nothing and no one falls between them.
