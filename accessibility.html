Accessibility statement

Last reviewed January 2, 2027

Compago serves people who are often failed by systems that weren't built with them in mind. We want this website to work for everyone who visits it, including people who use assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we've done

The site is built with plain, semantic HTML so it works with screen readers and keyboard navigation. It includes a skip link to the main content and visible focus outlines. Text and background colors are chosen to meet AA contrast. Images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers. Layouts adapt to small screens and to zoom up to 200 percent. Motion is kept to a minimum. The animated diagram on the homepage can be paused, and it stays still for visitors who have reduced motion turned on.

Known limitations

Our contact form is provided by Jotform, a third party, and we don't control all of its markup. If you have trouble with the form, email or call us and we'll take your request directly.

Feedback and help

If you run into a barrier on this site, or need information in a different format, please tell us. We'll reply within 2 business days.
