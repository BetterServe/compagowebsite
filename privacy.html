Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Compago Group LLC ("Compago," "we," "us"), collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers compagogroup.com only.

It does not cover information about people enrolled in the programs we run. That information is handled under our agreements with the organizations we work with and under applicable law, including HIPAA where it applies.

Information you give us

When you start a conversation through our contact form or reach us directly, we collect what you choose to share: your name, organization, work email, and what you tell us about your program. Our contact form is provided by Jotform, which processes submissions on our behalf. If you email or call us, we receive your contact details and whatever you include in your message.

Please don't send health information or details about individual members or patients through this website, the form, or email.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Netlify, records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the time of your visit. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure, and fix problems. Our fonts are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn't send your information to a font provider.

We don’t currently use analytics. If we add an analytics tool, we’ll name it here, describe what it collects, and explain how to opt out.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies. The embedded contact form from Jotform may set cookies needed for the form to work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though the form may not work without them.

How we use information

We use the information described above to respond to your request, schedule and hold conversations, follow up about your program, operate and secure the website, and meet legal obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and respond to you, such as Netlify (hosting), Jotform (forms), and our email provider, and only as needed for those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, our users, or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and request information for as long as we need it to respond to you and manage our relationship with your organization, and then delete it unless we are required to keep it longer.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you've given us through this website by emailing systems@compagogroup.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, and we will honor requests as the law requires.

Children

This website is intended for organizations and professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page.
