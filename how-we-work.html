Questions partners ask.

Are you a vendor?

Not in the usual sense. We own our part of the program and answer for its results. Sometimes that’s one function, sometimes all of it, but the measure is always your outcomes, not our hours.

What kinds of programs do you run?

Programs where people enroll to reach support that keeps them healthy: early childhood, nutrition, cash assistance, benefit enrollment such as SNAP, WIC, and LIHEAP, healthy homes, and scholarships and giving.

How fast can you launch?

We’re set up to stand programs up quickly, automate the systems around them, and perform from the start. We’ll give you a timeline when we scope your program together.

Who are you trying to reach?

Tell us about your program. We'll show you how we'd get people enrolled and keep them enrolled.
